I. — About
“Your sound and your brand already have a look. My job is to make sure everybody sees it.”

Alternate Exposures is Bryson Glass: a photographer and videographer shooting music videos, cover art and merch campaigns for rappers and clothing brands across Atlanta and the surrounding area.

No studio and no stock setups. Every shoot happens on location, whether that's your block, a rooftop, a parking deck at 2 a.m., or wherever the song or the collection belongs. I plan around your rollout, so the visuals are cut, colored and sized for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and your web store before the drop.

How it works

    We lock the concept, shot list and locations around Atlanta, then work backward from your release date.
