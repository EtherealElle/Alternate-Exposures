Legal

Privacy.

The short version: your details are used to reply to you about a shoot, and nothing else.

What this site collects

Only what you type into the booking form on the contact page: your name, artist or brand name, email address, phone number, Instagram handle, any link you share, your preferred date, budget range and your message. Nothing is collected if you just browse the site.

What it's used for

Replying to your inquiry, planning the shoot, and following up about the work. That's it. Your details are never sold, rented or shared for advertising, and you won't be added to a mailing list.

Who else can see it

Form messages are delivered by email through a form-handling service, which stores the message so it can be sent on. Bryson Glass is the only person who reads them. Nobody else is given access.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and videos in the gallery are played from YouTube or Vimeo only after you click one — those companies may record that request under their own privacy policies.

Photos and video of you

Work shot for you may appear in this portfolio, on Instagram, and in other promotional material unless you ask otherwise. If you'd rather your images stayed private, say so before the shoot and that's respected. If something is already published and you want it taken down, ask and it will be removed.

Keeping and deleting your details

Inquiries are kept while a project is active and for a reasonable period afterward for tax and record-keeping. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time, and for a copy of what's held, by emailing alternateexposures@gmail.com.

Under 18

This site isn't aimed at children. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should handle booking and sign off on any images being published.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Questions about any of this? Ask through the contact page.
